## 5. Privacy Policy & Data Handling Disclosures

We believe in user privacy and data transparency. Below is an exact accounting of how your data is handled:

### A. Data Categories & Local Storage
* **Health & Fitness Data:** Your profile information (age, gender, height, weight goals), weight logs, body fat percentages, workout routines, exercise sets, and daily food logs are stored **locally on your device** in a private SQLite/Room database.
* **No Proprietary Cloud Accounts Required:** The Application does not require creating a proprietary third-party account to track your fitness.

### B. Google Health Connect Synchronization
* If you explicitly grant permissions, the Application reads and writes fitness data (such as weight, nutrition, exercise sessions, and total calories burned) via Google Health Connect to synchronize with compatible apps (like Google Fit, Fitbit, or Samsung Health).
* You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time via your Android System Settings.

### C. AI Processing, Cloud Proxy & Third-Party Transmissions
* **Eatalyzer Cloud AI (Default Mode):** When using Eatalyzer Cloud AI, the Application uses anonymous Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, a device-scoped identifier, hashed network/device metadata, and Google Play purchase tokens to enforce rate limits, verify subscriptions/credit packs, prevent trial abuse, and track AI credit quota in Google Cloud Firestore. Food search queries, barcode lookups, meal photos, and AI Coach prompts (including summary nutrition/workout context needed to answer your coaching questions) are sent over HTTPS to our Firebase Cloud Functions backend and processed via Google Gemini. Non-personal food and barcode nutrition lookups may be cached in Firestore to speed up future searches.
* **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & Custom MCP Mode:** If you configure your own Google Gemini, OpenAI, or custom MCP server credentials, your AI prompts and food images are transmitted directly over HTTPS from your device to your chosen provider.
* **Android AppFunctions & Connected AI Assistants (e.g., Google Gemini):** On supported Android 16+ devices (for users with the Ad-Free upgrade or an active Cloud AI subscription), if you explicitly enable Eatalyzer FitLink in your system AI assistant's Connected Apps settings or approve an in-chat permission prompt, the assistant may invoke local AppFunctions on your behalf to search your catalogs, read requested daily nutrition/weight/workout/recovery summaries, and add or delete logs. Data returned to the assistant is transmitted and processed by your chosen assistant provider (such as Google Gemini) under that provider's terms and privacy policy. If Health Connect Auto Mode is enabled, logs created via the assistant may also sync to Google Health Connect. You can revoke assistant access at any time in your assistant's Connected Apps settings.
* We do not send your health or fitness logs to advertisers. Advertising SDK data is described separately below.

### D. Backups & User Exports
* The Application provides manual and automatic local backup tools that package your local database and preferences into a zip archive or CSV/JSON files. You retain full control and ownership over where you store or share these export files. In addition, if enabled at the operating system level, Android Auto Backup may back up application database and preference files (excluding API keys and sensitive tokens) to your personal Google Drive account.

### E. Advertising & Ad Privacy Choices
* **Advertising provider:** The ad-supported version uses Google AdMob to display and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares device IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. This SDK data is separate from your locally stored health and fitness logs.
* **Your health data:** We do not send your health or fitness logs to AdMob or use those logs to target advertising.
* **Your choices:** We use Google's User Messaging Platform to present applicable privacy messages. When available, open **Settings → Ad privacy choices** to review or change your advertising privacy choices, including applicable US state opt-out choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Advertising choices do not prevent you from using the app.
